# Rigid body examples

Each notebook runs in your browser, with nothing to install. Open one and choose *Run > Run All Cells*.

1. **01_pendulum**: model a body in CAD, pin it, and watch the energy error stay bounded.
2. **02_heavy_top**: the thesis benchmark, with its second-order convergence.
3. **03_double_pendulum**: chaos from two ball joints.
4. **04_tumbling_t_handle**: an object flips in free space, purely because of its inertia.
5. **05_springs**: a spring against its exact solution, then a cube bouncing and tumbling on a spring.
